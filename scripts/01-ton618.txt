TITLE: TON 618, The Black Hole That Breaks the Scale
KEYWORDS: black hole, quasar, accretion disk, galaxy
---
Imagine a monster so massive that our entire solar system would look like a speck beside it. This is TON 618, one of the most extreme objects ever found.

TON 618 is a quasar, a blazing beacon powered by a supermassive black hole at its center. Estimates put that black hole at tens of billions of times the mass of our Sun.

The light we see from it left billions of years ago. So when we look at TON 618, we are looking at the deep past. We will never see it as it is now.

And that is the strange truth of astronomy. Every point of light in the night sky is a message from the past, and some of those messages are still arriving.
